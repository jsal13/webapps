Schenkerian & Motivic Analysis

Analysis gives a listener a way to explain how musical structure unfolds beneath the surface. Ear training turns that understanding into active listening and recognition.

Schenkerian analysis

Schenkerian analysis distinguishes between foreground detail, middleground connections, and background structure. In a broad sense, it asks how local events are shaped by larger tonal tendencies and how a musical line is simplified into deeper structural levels.

Even in introductory form, this method helps explain why a piece can feel stable or unstable without reducing it to a single chord progression.

Motivic analysis

Motivic analysis focuses on repeated melodic or rhythmic ideas and traces how they transform across a work. A motive may appear in inversion, augmentation, fragmentation, or rhythmic disguise while still remaining recognizable.

Motivic connections often reveal the logic behind a piece's form and emotional arc.
