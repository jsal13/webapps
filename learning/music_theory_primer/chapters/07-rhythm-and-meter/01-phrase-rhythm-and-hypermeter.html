Hypermeter & Phrase Rhythm

Meter does not stop at the measure. It can organize larger spans of music, creating hypermeter and phrase-level rhythmic expectations.

Hypermeter

Hypermeter is a larger pattern of measures that behaves like a metric cycle beyond the single measure. For example, four bars may group into a larger pattern of strong and weak accents even when each bar carries the same meter.

The effect is often felt as a phrase's rhythmic shape: a phrase may begin with a strong downbeat, then continue with four-bar or eight-bar regularity before a cadence resolves the larger pattern.

Phrase rhythm

Phrase rhythm is the way musical phrases distribute emphasis and duration across time. A phrase may feel balanced or unbalanced depending on whether its melodic and metric accents line up with harmonic cadences and textual phrasing.

Composers often manipulate phrase rhythm to create momentum, anticipation, or a delayed sense of arrival.
