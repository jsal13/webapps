Polyrhythm, Polymeter, and Metric Modulation

As rhythmic complexity rises, the listener may hear multiple metric layers at once or experience one meter gradually turning into another.

Polyrhythm and polymeter

Polyrhythm occurs when different rhythmic durations are layered simultaneously, such as 3:2 patterns in a single texture. Polymeter occurs when two or more meters coexist as independent groupings, often producing a sense of friction or rotation.

These devices can create momentum, tension, and a more improvisatory or cross-cultural feel, especially in jazz, African music traditions, and contemporary composition.

Metric modulation

Metric modulation is the gradual reinterpretation of one pulse unit as another. One beat may be re-labeled as a different note value, causing the listener to hear a new metric framework without losing the original continuity of the music.

This is particularly common in twentieth-century and contemporary repertories where rhythmic continuity is as important as harmonic continuity.
